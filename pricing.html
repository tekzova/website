Why free

Because the alternative is worse.

An app that is free and takes your data is not free. These apps cost almost nothing to run, because they run on your phone.

No servers to pay for

Nothing of yours is stored anywhere, so there is no hosting bill to pass on to you.

A subscription would be dishonest

Questions

About the money.

Is there a catch?
No advertising, no analytics, no data collection, no upsell screen. The apps are free because they cost us almost nothing to run.
Will the free features ever move behind a paywall?
No. Anything that is free today stays free. A paid tier, if it appears, would only cover features that do not exist yet.
Why never a subscription?
You are not renting a service. The app runs on your phone with no server behind it, so charging monthly would be charging for nothing.
How would a refund work?
Full refund within 30 days of purchase, by email, without needing a reason. Until there is something to buy, this is a promise rather than a policy in force.
