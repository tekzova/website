Portfolio Tracker · Analyse

Know what is working — and what is not.

Portfolio Tracker does not stop at totals. It checks the basics, tests each fund against its index, and tells you what deserves attention next.

  • Money health — six checks on the dashboard
  • What to do next, most urgent first
  • Fund health, growth and a yearly review

Money health

Six checks, the moment you open it.

Before any chart, the dashboard tells you whether the basics are in place — each check green, amber or red, with the number behind it.

  • Emergency fund — months of spending and EMIs you could cover
  • Life cover and health cover against a rough need
  • Mix vs target — equity, debt and gold against the split you chose
  • Loan EMIs as a share of income, and your savings rate
What to do next

A to-do list written by your own numbers.

The app reads across everything you have entered and lists what deserves attention — most urgent first, each with a button that takes you straight to the fix.

  • A premium overdue, or a deposit maturing soon
  • A goal three years away that is still mostly in equity
  • A fund trailing its index, a regular plan, or funds that overlap
  • An endowment policy worth checking, nominees not recorded
  • Tax-year deadlines before 31 March

Not now? Hide an item for 30 days. These are prompts to review — never instructions to buy or sell.

Fund health

Would an index fund have done better?

Each fund is compared with the same money, on the same dates, put into its index instead — every purchase and every sale. It is the fairest test there is, and it takes your own timing into account.

  • Your XIRR beside the same money in Nifty 50, Nifty 100, Midcap 150 or Smallcap 250
  • A plain verdict — and "too new to judge" for funds under three years
  • Regular plans flagged, with the commission they carry
  • Funds that mostly buy the same companies, called out
  • Index history downloads once; nothing about you is sent
Growth & benchmarks

Your portfolio over the years — against the market.

Value and money invested, month by month or tax year by tax year, over one, three or five years or all of it. Lay an index over it to see how you really did.

  • Invested, withdrawn and realised gain for any period
  • Compare with Nifty 50, Nifty 100, Midcap 150 and Smallcap 250
  • Built from your own transactions and fund-price history
Yearly review

The once-a-year check-up, done for you.

One page that puts the year side by side: what you were worth a year ago and today, how the money did, and what needs a decision before the next one.

  • Where you stand: net worth now, a year ago, and the change
  • Money health and your mix against target
  • Every goal — on track, behind or reached
  • Every fund with a review note
  • Tax this year — and save it all as a PDF
