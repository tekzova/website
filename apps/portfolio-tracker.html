Where your money data lives

On your device. Not on ours — we don't have one.

Your statements are read on your phone or PC and never uploaded. There is no Tekzova account to create and no server holding your portfolio. The app goes online only when you tap for it — to fetch public fund prices, index history or the fund list.

Statements are read on the device

CAS, CAMS and MFCentral files — password-protected PDFs included — are opened and parsed right where they are.

Online only when you ask

Refresh NAV fetches the day's public fund prices. Turn on Fully offline mode and even that stops. Nothing about you is ever sent.

Lock it, hide it, back it up

An optional app password encrypts what is saved. Privacy mode hides every amount. Backups go to a file you keep — or a Google Drive or OneDrive folder you choose.

Is it for you?

Built for investors in Indian mutual funds — resident or NRI.

If you hold funds through several AMCs, keep FDs and PPF on the side, and want one honest picture without handing your logins to anyone, this is the app.

What it is

  • A tracker for everything you own — fed by statements you already download
  • A planner: goals, retirement, withdrawals and a monthly investment plan
  • A second pair of eyes: money health, fund health and insurance checks
  • Reports for filing season, for you and your tax adviser
  • A guide that explains every number it shows

What it is not

  • Not a trading app — it never buys or sells anything
  • Not linked to your broker, bank or AMC — no logins, no OTPs
  • Not a tip service — it shows facts and rules of thumb, not stock picks
  • Not tax or investment advice — check big decisions with a professional

Questions

Before you install.

Do I have to type in my funds?
No. Import the consolidated account statement (CAS) you can download from CAMS, KFintech or MFCentral and every fund, folio and transaction comes in at once. Stocks come in from your broker's holdings sheet, NPS from the CRA statement, and other assets from a simple CSV — or add them by hand.
Does it need my broker or bank login?
Never. It reads files you already have. It cannot place orders, move money or see your accounts.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything — imports, reports, planning, the guide — works with no connection. It goes online only when you tap for it: Refresh NAV for the latest public fund prices, and one-time downloads of index history and the fund list. Fully offline mode switches all of that off.
Can I use it on my phone and my PC?
Yes. Save a backup on one and open it on the other — the Android app and the Windows program use the same file. If you keep a Google Drive or OneDrive folder synced, point the app at it and backups land there automatically.
Can I track my spouse's and parents' money too?
Yes. Each family member gets a profile with their own portfolio, assets, SIPs and reports, and the family view adds everyone together.
Is it tax advice?
No. The capital-gains reports are worked out from your own statements and laid out the way the return asks for them, so filing is easier for you and your tax adviser. Rules change and circumstances differ — check the figures before you file.
What if I lose my phone?
Your data lives only where you keep it, so keep a backup. Restore that file on the new phone or on your PC and everything returns. The app also keeps its last five recovery points on the device.
