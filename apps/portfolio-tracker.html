What it works out

Returns done properly, not “current minus invested”.

Money went in on forty different dates. A single percentage is not an answer.

XIRR, three ways

Current for what you hold, lifetime including everything sold, and combined across the portfolio. Scheme XIRR counts every folio, including redeemed ones — where most calculations quietly go wrong.

Realised and unrealised, apart

Booked gains and paper gains are different questions with different tax consequences. Five distinct lines, not one blended number.

Allocation, three cuts

By asset class, by AMC, and scheme by scheme — so “am I diversified or do I own the same twelve companies five times?” has a chart rather than a feeling.

Growth, against a benchmark

Your value over time drawn at NAV rather than at cost, with NIFTY on the same axes. The honest version of “am I beating the index?”

Today’s change, dated

Latest NAVs when you ask for them, and the dashboard always shows which date the valuation is as of — so a stale number is visibly stale.

SIPs, detected not typed

The app spots the recurring pattern in your own transactions and proposes the SIP. Registered versus active, monthly or quarterly.

Questions

Before you install.

Do I have to give it my broker or fund login?
No, and there is nowhere to type one. It works from statement files you download yourself. If it never has your credentials, they cannot be lost with it.
My CAS PDF is password-protected. Is that a problem?
No. Enter the password when you import and the file is opened on the device. Neither the password nor the file goes anywhere else.
If it is offline, how do prices update?
Only when you tap Refresh NAV, and only to fetch published net asset values — nothing about your holdings is sent to get them. Fully offline mode removes even that.
Is this tax advice?
No. The reports arrange your own data into familiar formats, including ITR-style capital gains and Schedule 112A. Checking them is between you and your accountant.
Can my wife’s folios live in the same app?
Yes. Separate profiles, separate reports, and a combined family view. At import the app asks which profile a folio belongs to rather than guessing.
