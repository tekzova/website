Features

The PDF app you can run a meeting from.

Everything a PDF editor normally does, plus the presenting tools most of them leave out.

Present on a TV or projector

The slides fill the big screen while your phone shows the current and next page, a timer, a laser pointer and a blank-screen button.

Present to phones

With Audience Sync the slides appear on each person’s own phone and turn with yours. Nothing to install on their side.

Listen instead of reading

Read-aloud speaks a PDF or a Word file to you, from the start or from the text you select.

Opens Word and PowerPoint too

Word, text, Markdown and PowerPoint files open directly, ready to read, present or turn into a PDF.

Edit the pages

Reorder, rotate, delete, extract, split, merge, and insert pages or photos. Crop, number and watermark them.

Mark up and sign

Pen, highlighter, text notes, shapes and arrows. Draw your signature once and reuse it.

Presenting

No projector in the room? Use their phones.

Start Audience Sync and show two QR codes: one joins the Wi-Fi, the other opens the slides.

  • Pages turn on every phone when you turn yours
  • Zoom into a detail and their screens zoom to the same spot
  • Your laser dot and live pen marks show on their phones
  • They can look back at an earlier page, then tap Back to live
  • A live count shows how many phones are following

And the everyday tools

  • Photos to PDF, PDF to pictures, PDF to text
  • Reduce a PDF’s size; lock it with a password or remove one
  • Print chosen pages, odd or even, with every page made A4
  • Search, outline, thumbnails, night mode; it remembers your page
  • Make a scanned PDF searchable, and redact text (Pro)
  • Your original file is never changed — every tool saves a new copy

Questions

Before you install.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no server of ours that holds your files.
How does Audience Sync work without internet?
Your phone serves the slides over your own hotspot or a shared Wi-Fi network. People scan a QR code and the slides open in their phone’s browser. Mobile data can stay off.
Can people keep the slides?
Only if you allow it. You choose whether attendees may look ahead and whether they may save the PDF.
How do I turn pages while presenting?
Tap, swipe, the volume keys, a Bluetooth clicker or the TV remote.
Does it list every PDF on my phone?
No. The app does not ask for access to all your files. You add the files you want, or open one from WhatsApp, email or the Files app.
Which phones does it run on?
Android 8.0 and newer.
