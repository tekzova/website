Why it is different

Most money apps collect your data. This one can’t.

Your finances stay on your PC — not in someone else’s cloud

Statements are read and saved on your own device. There is no OffGridWealth server, so nothing of yours is ever uploaded to us. Choose offline mode, or switch on the two optional online features — refreshing fund prices and backing up to your own Google Drive — only when you want them.

See your whole picture at a glance

Net worth, your largest positions and your allocation in one dashboard. Years of spreadsheets become clear totals you can come back to any time.

Retirement estimates from your own numbers

Goals, FIRE and withdrawal projections built from what you actually hold — powerful planning tools without uploading your life to the cloud.

Work from data you already trust

No ads. No selling your data

Questions

Before you install.

Do I have to type in my funds?
No. Import the consolidated account statement (CAS) you can download from CAMS, KFintech or MFCentral, and every fund, folio and transaction comes in at once. Shares come from your broker’s holdings sheet, NPS from the CRA statement, and other assets from a simple CSV — or add them by hand.
Does it need my broker or bank login?
Never. It reads files you already have. It cannot place orders, move money or see your accounts.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Imports, reports, planning and the guide all work with no connection. It goes online only for things you switch on yourself: Refresh NAV for the day’s public fund prices, one-time downloads of index history and the fund list, and Google Drive backup if you connect it. Fully offline mode switches all of that off.
Can I use it on my phone and my PC?
Yes. Save a backup on one and open it on the other — the Android app and the Windows program use the same file. Connect your own Google Drive on both and they share one backup folder.
Can I track my spouse’s and parents’ money too?
Yes. Each family member gets a profile with their own portfolio, assets, SIPs and reports, and the family view adds everyone together.
Is it tax advice?
No. The capital-gains reports are worked out from your own statements and laid out the way the return asks for them, so filing is easier for you and your tax adviser. Rules change and situations differ — check the figures before you file.
What happens after 14 days?
Nothing you entered is lost. The core tracker stays free for good: imports, holdings with XIRR, every asset and loan, your net-worth number, the guide, the calculators and backups. Planning, the checks, family, tax reports and exporting your own reports come with Pro — one payment in the store you installed from. The Pro screens stay visible and run fully on the two sample families, so you can see what they would show.
Where do I get it?
Windows from the Microsoft Store and Android from Google Play — both free, with every feature unlocked for the first 14 days. Every screen is shown on the app’s own website, offgridwealth.in.
