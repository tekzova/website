Investor Guide

A book on Indian investing, inside the app.

Thirty-nine chapters on everything an investor in India — resident or NRI — should know before and while investing: how each kind of investment works, what it costs, how it is taxed, what can go wrong, and how to keep a portfolio balanced over the years.

  • Learn — how everything works, chapter by chapter
  • Do — step-by-step workbooks
  • Plan — answer a few questions and get your own plan
  • Search the whole guide; it remembers what you have read
  • Save any part as a PDF to read away from the screen
Plain words

Each chapter starts with the short version.

Each chapter opens with In a nutshell — the whole idea in three sentences — then goes deeper for those who want it. Rules and rates are dated, so you know how current they are.

Glossary

173 terms, explained for a beginner.

XIRR, NAV, exit load, indexation, LTCG, sum assured, surrender value. Every term has three parts:

  • In simple words — what it means, without jargon
  • Example — with real rupee amounts
  • What to do — how it changes your next step

Any underlined word in the guide opens its glossary entry with a tap — and a Back button takes you to where you were reading.

On every screen

Not sure what a number means? Tap the ⓘ.

Every card in the app has a small info button that explains the number in front of you — what XIRR is, why the emergency fund counts EMIs, how the life-cover estimate is worked out.
