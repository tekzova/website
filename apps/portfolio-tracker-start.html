The first minute

Three steps, and a sample family to try first.

The first time you open it, the app shows what it does and offers three steps:

Import your statement

Drop in a CAS, CAMS or MFCentral file. Funds, folios and every transaction come in at once.

Add what else you own

Shares, deposits, EPF, PPF, NPS, gold, property, loans — once, with the fields that matter.

Build your plan

Answer a planner's questions and get a monthly amount, a mix and your goals.

Not ready with your own files? Tap Try with sample data. Two made-up but carefully built families load in, so every screen has something to show. Remove them whenever you like — they are never counted in your own totals.

Which file?

Getting your statement.

The most complete start is a consolidated account statement (CAS) covering CAMS and KFintech, with the full transaction history. Ask for the detailed version, from your first investment to today, on the CAMS, KFintech or MFCentral website. It arrives by email, usually as a password-protected PDF — Portfolio Tracker opens those.

Filing this year? Also download the MFCentral capital-gains report for that financial year.

Phone ⇄ PC

One backup file, on both devices.

There is no account and no sync server, so you decide where copies live.

  • Backup to a file — plain or compressed — and restore it on the phone or the PC
  • Cloud folder backup: point the app at a Google Drive or OneDrive folder your computer already syncs; the app itself uploads nothing
  • On a PC, a database file keeps a live copy on disk that updates with every change
  • The last five recovery points are kept automatically, before anything big changes
Your settings

Lock it down, or keep it simple.

  • App password — encrypts everything the app saves; asked on every launch
  • Privacy mode — hides every amount at a tap
  • Fully offline mode — no fund-price or index downloads at all
  • Dark or light theme
  • Optional modules — switch off what you do not use and the menus get shorter

An app password cannot be recovered if it is forgotten — there is no server to reset it from. Keep it somewhere safe.
