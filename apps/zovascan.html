Document scanner · Android

Scan it. Keep it on your phone.

Most scanner apps want an account and send your papers to their servers. ZovaScan does the whole job on the phone: it finds the page, flattens it, cleans it and saves a sharp PDF — with no login and no uploads.

  • No account, no uploads, no ads
  • Works offline, from the first scan
  • You press the shutter — nothing is captured by itself

How it works

From a phone photo to a clean PDF.

Two short loops show what happens after you press the shutter.

Features

A scanner that treats your papers as yours.

Bills, certificates, ID cards, notes and whole books — scanned, tidied and filed on the phone in your hand.

Pages that come out flat

The app finds the page, straightens it and levels the lines of text, so a photo of a curled sheet reads like a scan.

Filters for every kind of paper

Clear, Docs, black and white, newspaper, handwriting, whiteboard and photo. Keep the paper colour, or make the background white.

Many pages, one PDF

Scan a whole file in one go, reorder or delete pages, and save as a PDF or as JPG pictures.

A Vault for ID cards

Keep ID cards and sensitive papers behind your fingerprint or a PIN, encrypted on the phone.

Read the text

Pull the words out of a scan to copy or share. Reading happens on the phone.

QR codes and barcodes

Read a code with the camera, or make your own QR code to print or share.

Real screens

Simple from the first screen.

The app’s own screens. Tap one to enlarge it.

Five ways to scan

One page, a book, or a stack.

Pick how you are scanning and the app adjusts to it.

  • Document for a single sheet
  • Batch for a stack, page after page
  • Book for bound pages
  • ID card for both sides on one sheet
  • Photo for pictures and passport-size photos

Pro tools, when you need them

  • Sign and mark up a document, with saved signatures
  • PDF tools: merge, split, organise, page numbers, watermark, compress
  • Password on a PDF, and searchable PDFs
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint files to PDF
  • Backup to your own Google Drive, encrypted, when you tap Back up
  • App lock, the Crisp filter, Count and Measure

Every Pro tool is free for 14 days. After that, one payment keeps them — never a subscription.

The part you can verify

Check what the app is allowed to do.

ZovaScan asks for the camera, and for internet access only so you can back up to your own Google Drive.

It does not ask for your contacts, your location or access to every file on the phone. You add only the pictures and PDFs you choose.

Questions

Before you install.

Does it upload my documents?
No. Finding the page, straightening it, filters and text reading all run on the phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The only connection the app makes is to your own Google Drive, and only when you tap Back up.
Do I need an account?
No. Open the app and scan. There is no sign-up, no login and no advertising.
Does it take the picture by itself?
No. A page is saved only when you press the shutter, so you decide what is captured.
What is free and what is Pro?
Scanning, straightening, all filters except Crisp, folders, search, PDF and JPG, sharing, printing, text reading in English and QR codes are free with no limits. The Pro tools are free for 14 days, then one payment if you want to keep them. Never a subscription.
Which phones does it run on?
Android 8.0 and newer.
