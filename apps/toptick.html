Features

A proper checklist, not a notes app with tick boxes.

Built by people who kept getting home and realising.

Sixteen lists, already written

Grocery, travel packing, ship joining kit, exam prep, vehicle service, daily goals and more — 998 suggested items in tidy groups.

Quantities that make sense

Tap the pill, set the number with a stepper, and the unit is already right — kg, L, lb, gal, dozen, count.

US or metric, your call

One switch changes the units and the suggestions. Six lists swap their local items to match.

Reminders that turn up

Once, daily, weekly or monthly — and they survive switching the phone off and on.

Lists that start again

A repeating list clears itself each day, week or month and files the finished one in History.

Yours to keep and to lock

A PIN at start-up if you want one, dark and light themes, and one-tap backup to a file you own.

Making a list

Three ways to build a list. Most apps give you one.

Tap what you need

Open an activity and the items are already there in labelled groups — produce, dairy, frozen, household. A full grocery run takes about forty seconds, and no typing.

Paste a sentence, get a checklist

Someone texts “bread, eggs, coffee, dish soap, bananas”. Copy it, open TopTick, tap once: five separate, tickable items. Commas, bullets, numbered lines and line breaks are all understood.

Start blank, or import someone else’s

Type your own when the job is unusual — or import a list someone shared, and it becomes a real checklist with quantities, units and an icon.

Sharing

Send the list. They tick. You see it.

Sharing is a plain WhatsApp or email message — readable by anyone, even without the app. If they do have TopTick, something better happens.

  • They import it and tick items off in their own aisle
  • They send it back and your list updates instead of making a duplicate
  • Their ticks arrive with the time each was done and who did it — nothing you ticked is undone

Questions

Before you install.

If it has no internet, how does sharing work?
Your phone’s share sheet does it. TopTick hands the text to WhatsApp, Gmail or whatever you pick, and that app sends it — the same as sharing a photo.
What happens to my lists if I change phones?
Backup to file writes everything to one small file you keep wherever you like. On the new phone, Restore brings back every list, tick, quantity and reminder.
Can two of us work on the same list?
Yes. Share it, they tick items in their own copy and send it back, and your list updates rather than duplicating.
Does it work offline?
It only works offline. Creating, ticking, reminders and backups all happen on the device, with no signal at all.
Which Android versions does it run on?
Android 7.0 and newer. The app is about 4 MB.
What will it cost?
The core is free for good. Advanced features will unlock for 14 days to try, then one payment if you want to keep them — never a subscription.
