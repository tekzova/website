Features

A proper checklist, not a notes app with tick boxes.

Sixteen lists, already written

Grocery, packing, ship joining kit, exam prep, vehicle service, daily goals. 998 items in tidy groups — tap what you want, done in under a minute.

Quantities that make sense

Beef in lb, milk by the gallon, eggs by the dozen. Tap the pill, set the number, the unit is already right.

US or metric, your call

One switch in Settings. Six lists swap their local items to match — pounds and gallons, or kilos and litres.

Reminders that turn up

Once, daily, weekly or monthly — and they survive a reboot, which is exactly where most reminder apps quietly fail.

Lists that restart themselves

Set one to repeat and each day, week or month it clears — filing the finished one in History. Daily goals become a record.

Yours to keep and to lock

A PIN at startup if you want one. Dark and light themes. One-tap backup to a file you own.

Making a list

Three ways in. Most apps give you one.

Typing forty items with two thumbs is why shopping lists end up on the back of an envelope.

Tap what you need

Open an activity and the items are already there in labelled groups. A full grocery run takes about forty seconds and no typing at all.

Paste a sentence, get a checklist

Someone texts “bread, eggs, coffee, dish soap”. Copy, open TopTick, tap once — four separate tickable items. Commas, bullets and numbered lines are all understood.

Start blank, or import theirs

Type your own when the job is unusual, or take a list somebody shared and it becomes a real checklist — quantities, units and icon included.

Sharing

Share a list, get their ticks back.

You share it

Tap Share and pick WhatsApp, email, anything. It goes out as ordinary text carrying the activity’s own icon.

They tick it on their phone

They copy the message, tap Import, and it is a real checklist in their app — quantities and units and all.

Your list updates

They send it back and TopTick recognises it as the same list. Their ticks appear on your copy with the time each was done. Nothing you already ticked is undone.

Nothing sits on a server waiting to be shared, so your shopping list is never in a database waiting to be leaked.

Questions

Before you install.

If it has no internet, how does sharing work?
Your phone’s share sheet does it. TopTick hands the text to WhatsApp or Gmail and that app sends it — the same as sharing a photo. TopTick never opens a connection, which is why it needs no permission.
Can two people work on the same list?
Yes. Share it, they tick items in their copy, send it back, and your list updates rather than duplicating. It also keeps working in a shop with no signal.
What happens if I change phones?
Settings → Backup to file writes everything to one small file you keep. Restore on the new phone brings back every list, tick, quantity and reminder.
Which Android versions?
Android 7.0 (2016) and newer. The app is about 4 MB.
Will it start costing money?
Every feature here is free with no account and no time limit. Advanced features, if added, would be a one-time purchase — never a subscription. How pricing works.
