Portfolio Tracker · Track

Everything you own, in one place.

Import the statements you already have, add the rest once, and Portfolio Tracker keeps the whole picture — for you or the whole family.

  • CAS, CAMS, MFCentral, broker and NPS files in
  • 23 kinds of holding, plus loans and cards
  • Family profiles with a combined view

Import

Feed it the statements you already download.

No typing in fund by fund. One consolidated account statement brings in every fund, folio and transaction you hold across AMCs — years of history in a few seconds.

  • CAMS + KFintech consolidated CAS (PDF) — every AMC, full history
  • CAMS ActiveStatement (PDF) and MFCentral CAS (Excel)
  • MFCentral capital-gains report — the exact figures your registrar worked out
  • Stocks from your broker’s holdings sheet, NPS from the CRA statement
  • Fifteen other asset classes in bulk from a simple CSV, with a template to start from
  • Password-protected PDFs open right in the app

Every file is read on your device and never uploaded. Statements are checked against the owner's PAN and name, so a spouse's CAS does not land in your profile by mistake, and importing the same NPS statement twice adds nothing.

Holdings

Every fund, down to the last instalment.

Open any holding and see what it is really doing — not just today's value.

  • Current value, money invested and the gain in rupees and per cent
  • Present return on units you hold and total return including what you already withdrew
  • Current XIRR and lifetime XIRR — the yearly return that allows for when each rupee went in
  • Units, average cost, latest NAV and the 1-day change
  • Every purchase still held: date, units, price, value now, gain and days held — handy for tax
All assets

Not just funds. Everything.

Net worth is more than a mutual-fund statement. Add each thing you own once, with the fields that matter for it — an FD's rate and maturity, a PPF account's yearly deposits, a property's purchase price, a policy's premium.

And on the other side: home loans, car loans, personal loans and credit-card dues, with their EMIs and end dates.

Net worth

One number — and what is inside it.

Everything you own minus everything you owe, grouped the way you think about it, with the change since last month.

  • Where it is: funds, equity, deposits and small savings, retirals, gold, property, insurance, cash
  • Emergency fund in months of spending and EMIs
  • A protection check — life and health cover against a rough need
  • Your mix against the target you set, with a nudge only when it drifts
  • Every loan and card with its EMI, and the total going out each month
  • Net worth over time, with a dotted five-year projection
Family

The whole household, together or one by one.

Give each family member a profile — their own funds, assets, SIPs and reports. Then switch on the family view to see everyone added up.

  • Family net worth, split by member
  • Household goals funded by more than one person — a child’s education from both parents
  • Every screen and report works for one member or the whole family
  • Two sample families built in to explore — never counted in your own totals
SIPs

Each SIP, with its own report card.

Let the app find your SIPs in your transactions, or add them by hand. Each one shows how many instalments have gone in, what they are worth and the return they have earned.

  • Monthly amount, start date, instalments, invested and current value
  • XIRR for each SIP and for all of them together
  • Mark a SIP stopped when you stop it — history stays
Transactions & charts

Every transaction — searchable.

All your transactions in one list, filtered by type and searchable by fund or folio. Charts show the mix by asset class, by AMC and by scheme, against the target you set.

  • Buys, sells and IDCW payouts straight from your statements
  • Allocation pie with what you own, what you invested and the gain
  • Date-wise mutual-fund transaction report, ready for Excel
And the small things

Details that save an evening.

Labels

Your own tags — Retirement, Emergency, a child’s name — on any fund or asset, with totals per label.

Privacy mode

One tap hides every amount, for when someone is looking over your shoulder.

Refresh NAV

Fetch the latest public fund prices when you choose. Statements alone work too.

Find a fund

Search every Indian mutual fund and SIF from a list downloaded once and kept on your device.

Only what you use

Switch off modules you do not need — the menus get shorter, the app stays simple.

Recovery points

The app keeps your last five snapshots automatically, before anything big changes.
