Portfolio Tracker · Plan

A plan built from your real numbers.

Most planning tools start from a blank form. Portfolio Tracker starts from what you actually own — so the plan, the goals and the retirement date all move as your money does.

  • Plan Builder — risk profile, monthly amount and mix
  • Goals that track themselves
  • FIRE and SWP planners, seven calculators

Plan Builder

The questions a planner asks. Your answers. Your plan.

Plan Builder walks through what a financial planner would ask in a first meeting — and then does the arithmetic a planner would do. Tap Fill from my portfolio and most answers come straight from your holdings, loans, insurance, SIPs and goals.

  • About you: age, retirement age, family, dependants — resident, NRI or seafarer
  • Your work and how steady the income is
  • Monthly cash flow: income, spending, EMIs and SIPs running now
  • Six short questions about how you handle risk

Out comes a plan: your risk profile, what is free each month, a suggested monthly investment, a long-term mix of equity, debt and gold — and a health check that puts the costly things first, like card dues at 36–42% a year or a life cover that falls short.

One tap uses the mix as your target, creates the goals, or saves the whole plan as a PDF.

Goals

Name what the money is for.

Retirement, a child's education, a house without a loan, an emergency fund, a car in 2029. Give each goal a cost in today's money and a date, link the funds and assets that pay for it, and the app does the rest.

  • A progress ring, and whether you are on track or behind
  • What it needs each month, what is going in now, and the date you reach it on this pace
  • The target grown for inflation, so the number is real on the day
  • A warning when money needed within three years is still mostly in equity
  • Can you fund all of them at once? — every goal added up against what you invest
One goal, up close

See exactly what is funding it.

Open a goal and every fund, EPF, PPF, NPS account and SIP linked to it is listed with its value. Drag a slider to see what a bigger monthly amount does to the finish date.

  • Target today, corpus at the date, and growth assumed
  • What-if: add more a month and watch the date move
  • Household goals shared between family members
Retirement & FIRE

When can you stop working?

The retirement planner works out your FI number — the corpus that covers your spending for life — and the age you reach it on what you invest now. Then it runs your money year by year to the age you plan for.

  • FI number in today’s money, the year you get there and how far along you are
  • Corpus needed at retirement, spent down to your planning age
  • Lean, Fat, Coast and Barista FIRE — five finish lines, side by side
  • Simple mode for a quick answer, Advanced for every assumption
  • A plan report and a year-by-year projection to print or keep
Withdrawals (SWP)

How long will the money last?

Plan a monthly withdrawal from a corpus — your net worth, or one goal's money — and see the year it runs out, or the most you could take each month and still last the distance.

  • Withdrawals that rise each year with inflation
  • Month-by-month table: opening, withdrawn, growth, closing
  • Send the result to the retirement planner as income
  • Track the SWPs you already take
