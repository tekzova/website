The count

The rule is simple. Applying it for eleven years is not.

Everyone knows the headline. The trouble is the edges — which side of midnight a travel day falls on, and what the previous decade looked like.

The day-count rules NRE Tracker applies
What the app appliesHow it counts
Financial year1 April to 31 March
Non-resident for the yearUnder 182 days present in India
The day you leave IndiaCounted as an India day
The day you returnCounted as an abroad day
A contract crossing 31 MarchSplit across both years
An open contractCounted to today, re-counted tomorrow

The travel-day convention is the rule people get wrong most often by hand, and it is worth two days a trip.

RNOR

The year you come home is the one worth getting right.

Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident is decided by the ten years behind you, not the one you are in.

Condition A — nine of the last ten

Each of the ten preceding years is re-counted from your own records and marked resident or non-resident. The app tells you which ones broke the run.

Condition B — 729 days across seven

A single running total across seven financial years — almost impossible to do reliably on paper, and trivially easy to get wrong by one trip.

Eleven years, one screen

One row per financial year — India days, abroad days, and the status that came out of them. Your whole record, scrollable.

Questions

Before you install.

Is this tax advice?
No, and it does not pretend to be. It counts days and applies published thresholds to your own records. It does not file anything and cannot account for your circumstances. Treat the output as a well-kept notebook to take to your accountant.
How does it treat the day I fly?
The day you leave India counts as an India day; the day you arrive back counts as an abroad day. Applying that consistently is usually worth two days per trip.
I have fifteen years of contracts in a spreadsheet. Do I retype them?
No. Import them. The app reads an Excel file and shows you the exact column headers it expects before you upload, so you can fix the sheet first.
Does it need internet?
Never, after you install it. Every count, report, import and backup happens on the device.
Why is it not on Google Play?
It is working and in daily use, but not yet through store review. The APK here is the same build, straight from us.
