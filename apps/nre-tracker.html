01 / The count

The rule is simple. Applying it for eleven years is not.

Everyone knows the headline: fewer than 182 days in India in a financial year. The trouble is the edges — which side of midnight a travel day falls on, where a year boundary cuts a contract, and what the previous decade looked like. Those are the parts the app does for you.

What the app appliesHow it counts
Financial year1 April to 31 March
Non-resident for the yearUnder 182 days present in India
The day you leave IndiaCounted as an India day
The day you return to IndiaCounted as an abroad day
A contract crossing 31 MarchSplit across both financial years
An open contractCounted up to today, and re-counted tomorrow

The travel-day convention above is the one applied to seafarers. It is the single rule people get wrong most often by hand, and it is worth two days a trip.

03 / RNOR

The year you come home is the one worth getting right.

Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident is decided by the ten years behind you, not the one you are in. Both conditions need a history nobody keeps — so the app keeps it, and evaluates them for every year at once.

Condition A

Non-resident in nine of the last ten years

Each of the ten preceding financial years is re-counted from your own voyage records and marked resident or non-resident. The app tells you how many of them qualify, and which ones broke the run.

Evaluated year by year, not assumed
Condition B

729 India days or fewer across the last seven

A single running total across seven financial years — the sort of sum that is almost impossible to do reliably on paper, and trivially easy to get wrong by one trip.

Totalled from the same voyage data

Behind both is an eleven-year table — one row per financial year, India days, abroad days, and the status that came out of them. Scroll it and your whole record is on one screen.

04 / The records

The day count is the output. These are the inputs.

Two registers sit behind every number on the dashboard, and both are useful on their own — one for the tax question, one for the next job application.

1

Voyages

Vessel, sign-on date, sign-off date. That is the whole entry. A contract with no sign-off yet is marked ongoing and counts up to today. Everything else — the year totals, the limit, the RNOR history — is derived from this one list.

2

Sea service, the CDC record

The same contracts kept the way a discharge book keeps them: vessel, rank, dates, days served. Ranks run from Trainee Seaman and Deck Cadet through Fourth, Third, Second and Chief Engineer, Electrical Officer, Master, Bosun, Fitter, Oiler, Pumpman and the rest.

3

Rank experience, added up

Total sea service, and time served in each rank, summarised automatically. The answer to “how long have you sailed as Second Engineer?” stops being an evening with a calculator.

4

Your profile, once

Name, date of birth, nationality, passport number, CDC number, PAN and rank are entered once and appear in the header of every report the app generates.

07 / Questions

Before you install.

Is this tax advice?
No, and it does not pretend to be. NRE Tracker counts days and applies published day-count thresholds to your own records. It does not file anything, does not know your income, and cannot account for your particular circumstances. Treat its output as a well-kept notebook to take to your accountant, not as a determination.
How does it treat the day I fly?
The day you leave India counts as an India day; the day you arrive back counts as an abroad day. This is the convention applied to seafarers, and applying it consistently is usually worth two days per trip against counting by hand.
I have fifteen years of contracts in a spreadsheet. Do I retype them?
No. Import them. The app reads an Excel file — all records at once from a matrix sheet, or voyages and sea service separately — and shows you the exact column headers it expects before you upload, so you can fix the sheet first.
Does it need internet?
Never, after you install it. Every count, report, import and backup happens on the device. It works on a ship with no signal, which is rather the point.
What happens when I change phones?
Export the JSON backup on the old phone, copy the file across, import it on the new one. Voyages, sea service records and your profile all come back.
Why is it not on Google Play?
It is working, in daily use, and not yet through store review. Until it is, the APK on this page is the app — the same build, straight from us. When it reaches Play, this page will carry the store link instead.
