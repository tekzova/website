The count

The rule is simple. Applying it for eleven years is not.

Everyone knows the headline number. The trouble is the edges — which side of midnight a travel day falls on, and what the last decade looked like. These are the rules the app applies, every time, without a slip.

The travel-day convention is the one people most often get wrong by hand — and it is worth two days a trip.

RNOR

The year you come home is the one worth getting right.

Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident status depends on the years behind you, not just the one you are in. The app re-counts each of them from your own records.

  • Nine of the last ten — each of the ten earlier years marked resident or non-resident, and which ones broke the run
  • 729 days across seven years — one running total, near impossible to keep on paper
  • Eleven years on one screen — India days, abroad days and the status for each

Not tax advice

NRE Tracker counts days and applies published thresholds to your own records. It does not file anything and cannot know your full circumstances — treat it as a well-kept notebook to take to your accountant.

The records

The day count is the output. These are the inputs.

Two registers sit behind every number — one for the tax question, one for the next job application.

Voyages

Vessel, sign-on and sign-off. Every count is worked out from this one list.

Sea service (CDC)

Vessel, rank, dates and days served — a register that matches your discharge book.

Name, date of birth, nationality, passport, CDC and PAN — entered once, printed on every report.

Questions

Before you install.

Is it really free?
Yes — every feature, on Android and Windows. NRE Tracker has no paid version at all.
Is this tax advice?
No, and it does not pretend to be. It counts days and applies published thresholds to your own records. Take the output to your accountant.
How does it treat the day I fly?
The day you leave India counts as an India day; the day you arrive back counts as an abroad day. Applied consistently, that is usually worth two days per trip.
I have fifteen years of contracts in a spreadsheet. Do I retype them?
No. Import them. The app reads an Excel file and shows the column headings it expects before you upload, so you can fix the sheet first.
Does it need the internet?
Never. Every count, report, import and backup happens on the device.
Where will I get it?
The Android app will be on Google Play, and the Windows program will download from this site. Leave your email and we will tell you the day it is live.
