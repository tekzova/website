Reports, not tax advice. Portfolio Tracker works these figures out from your own statements and lays them out the way the return asks for them, so filing is quicker for you and your chartered accountant. Tax rules change and every situation is different — check the figures, or have your CA check them, before you file.
Tax year at a glance

The tax year ends on 31 March. The app counts down.

A card on the dashboard keeps the year in view: how many days are left, the long-term equity gains you have already booked, and how much of the yearly tax-free allowance is still unused.

  • Days left to 31 March
  • Long-term equity gains booked so far this year
  • Tax-free allowance left — shown to the rupee, ₹1,25,000 a year
  • Long-term gains you could book now, on units held over 12 months

If you have gains to spare, it points out that selling and reinvesting up to the allowance before 31 March can use this year's limit and raise your cost price — and reminds you to check exit loads and the ELSS lock-in first.

Capital gains — summary

Every sale this year, fund by fund.

Pick a financial year. The summary lists each fund you sold from: units sold, sale value, cost, and the short-term and long-term gain — worked out first-in, first-out from your imported transactions.

Capital gains — ITR format

Sale by sale, the working your return needs.

Each sale split into the lots it came from, with sale date and price, purchase date and price, cost and gain — short-term and long-term in separate sections. It is the working you want open while filling in the capital-gains schedule.

Schedule 112A

Long-term equity sales, in the schedule's own columns.

Long-term sales of equity funds for the year, laid out in the column order of Schedule 112A — units, sale price, full value of consideration, cost of acquisition, deductions and balance — so rows can be copied across, then checked.

  • Save as PDF to share with your CA
  • Open in Excel to sort, check and copy
Unrealised gains

Know the tax before you sell.

Every open purchase lot with its unrealised gain, split short-term and long-term as on today's prices. Useful when you are deciding which units to sell — or whether to wait a few weeks for a lot to turn long-term.

Tax-saving tracker

What is used and what is left, in each limit.

Everything you have already put into deductible investments this financial year, pulled from your holdings: ELSS, PPF, EPF, Sukanya Samriddhi, life premiums, tax-saver FDs and NSC against the 80C limit, NPS against 80CCD(1B), and health premiums against 80D — each with what is still left.

These deductions apply under the old tax regime, and the report says so.

Registrar figures

Or use your registrar's own numbers.

Import the MFCentral capital-gains report for the filing year and the capital-gains reports are built from the registrar's own computed figures instead. Every report states which source it used, so you always know where a number came from.
