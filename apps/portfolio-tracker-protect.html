Portfolio Tracker · Protect & pass on

Cover the people who depend on you.

Investing is half the job. Portfolio Tracker also checks your insurance, tells you what a policy really earns, reminds you before a premium lapses and helps you leave things in order.

  • Insurance check — life and health cover
  • ULIP & endowment returns vs FD, PPF, bonds
  • Premium reminders, nominees and a will draft

Insurance check

Enough cover for the people who depend on you?

A rough, honest estimate from your own numbers — not a sales pitch for a bigger policy.

  • Life cover needed: your household spending for the years your family would need it, plus loans to clear and goals to protect, minus investments they could use
  • Health cover: a suggested family floater for your city, and a cheap super top-up for a big bill
  • Every policy listed with its cover, premium and end date — and a verdict on each
  • All premiums together, as a share of income
ULIP & endowment check

What does that policy really earn?

Endowment, money-back and ULIP plans mix insurance with investment, and the return is rarely printed on the brochure. Enter the premium, the term and the maturity value from your policy bond or benefit illustration, and the app works out the yearly return the policy actually earns.

Then it puts the same premiums, on the same dates, into the alternatives — and shows what each would be worth at maturity, and how much life cover comes with it:

Bank FDPPFPost office NSCPost office time depositRBI Floating Rate Savings BondGovernment bondTerm plan + index fundTerm plan + PPF
  • After-tax figures, with the assumptions on screen to change
  • Continue or stop? — the premiums still to pay against the surrender or paid-up value today
  • An NRI version that leaves out what NRIs cannot open
Premium reminders

A lapsed policy is a lost policy.

Premiums due in the next few weeks sit on the dashboard. An overdue one turns red and stays at the top of the action list until you mark it paid.

  • Mark paid moves the due date on by the policy’s frequency and adds to the total paid
  • Add to calendar saves a reminder to your phone or Outlook calendar — repeating every year, with alerts 7 days and 1 day before
  • Limited-pay policies stop reminding when the paying term ends
  • Add every policy in one go
Estate & Will

A will draft from what you actually own.

Your assets are already in the app, so the draft starts filled in. Choose beneficiaries and shares, name executors and witnesses, and a checklist shows what is still missing before you print.

  • Executor and reserve executor, two witnesses, a residuary beneficiary
  • Warnings for a witness who inherits, or a minor beneficiary
  • Open the draft and save it as a PDF, with a signing page that explains how to sign it
  • A plain reminder: this is a draft — have it looked at by a lawyer before you sign
Nominations & papers

Make it easy for your family to claim.

A list of every fund, account and policy with its nominee — and the ones with none recorded, so you can fix them. A second tab records where each paper is kept: the policy bond, the property deed, the locker key.

  • Nominee and relationship for every holding
  • A count of what has no nominee, and how much money that is
  • Print the list for the family file
  • The rules in plain words — nominee or heir, who actually gets the money
