Privacy policy

NRE Tracker collects nothing.

Voyages, sea service and profile details stay in the app’s own storage. The app makes no network requests.

NRE Tracker does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays in the app’s own storage on your device, and the app makes no network requests.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party analytics SDK in the app. We do not know that you installed it, and we cannot see anything you put into it.

What the app holds, and where

NRE Tracker stores the information you type into it — and nothing else:

  • Voyage records — vessel name, sign-on and sign-off dates.
  • Sea service records — vessel, rank and dates, as a CDC-style register.
  • Profile details — the name, date of birth, nationality, passport number, CDC number, PAN and rank you choose to enter so they appear on generated reports. Every one of these fields is optional; the day count works without them.

All of it is written to your device’s private app storage and read back from there. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and there is no server involved at any point.

Files you import and export

Excel files you import are read on the device using your own file picker. Reports (Excel and PDF), and JSON backups, are written only when you ask for them, to a location you choose. We never see any of these files and have no means of reaching them.

Permissions

The app requests only what Android requires to let you open a file you selected and save a file you asked for. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or phone permissions, and it does not run in the background.

Deleting your data

The app has a control that deletes everything it holds, immediately and on the device. Uninstalling the app also removes its storage. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request the deletion of from us.

What the app is not

NRE Tracker counts days and applies published day-count thresholds to the records you enter. It does not provide tax, legal or financial advice, does not file anything on your behalf, and cannot account for your individual circumstances. Its output is a record to take to a qualified adviser, not a determination of your residential status.

Children

NRE Tracker is a professional record-keeping utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that involves a network connection, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the change described plainly here.
