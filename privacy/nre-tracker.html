Privacy policy

Sea Service & NRE Tracker privacy policy.

Applies to Sea Service & NRE Tracker for Android (package com.tekzova.nretracker) and Windows. Last updated 21 November 2026.

The short version: Sea Service & NRE Tracker has no account and no server of ours. Everything you enter stays on your own device. The app goes online only if you choose to back up to your own Google Drive.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no Tekzova account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no advertising code in the app. We never see your records.

Where your data lives

Your profile (name, date of birth, nationality, passport, CDC and PAN details you choose to enter), your voyages, sea-service records and settings are written to your own device’s private storage and read back from it. None of it leaves the device unless you personally export a report or a backup and send it somewhere.

Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy
InternetOnly for the Google Drive backup and restore that you start yourself.
NotificationsFor the date reminders you switch on: your latest safe return date, the NRE deadline and 31 March.
Run at startupAndroid discards reminders when a phone restarts, so the app sets them again after a reboot.
Storage (older Android only)To save reports and backups where you choose.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or SMS permissions. On Windows, the program reads and writes only the files you pick.

Backup to Google Drive

Backup to Google Drive is optional. When you use it, you sign in with your own Google account on the phone’s own account screen, and the backup file goes to your own Drive. The app can see only the files it saved there. Google’s privacy policy applies to what is stored in your Drive. We have no access to it.

Reports and backups

Excel and PDF reports, and backup files, are created only when you ask for them and saved to a location you pick. Your profile details are printed in the header of reports because that is what the reports are for; nothing is sent to us.

Not advice

The app counts days and applies published thresholds to the records you enter. It does not file anything, cannot know your full circumstances, and is not tax advice.

Children

Sea Service & NRE Tracker is a tool for working seafarers and travellers. It is not directed at children and collects no data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever uses a network connection in a new way, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described plainly here.
