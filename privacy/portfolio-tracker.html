Privacy policy

Portfolio Tracker never asks for a login.

Statements are parsed on your device. The one request the app can make asks for public prices and carries nothing about your holdings.

Portfolio Tracker does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal or financial data. It never asks for a broker, fund-house or bank login. Your holdings are parsed and kept on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party analytics SDK. We do not know who uses the app or what is in it.

No credentials, ever

The app has no field anywhere for a broker, fund-house, registrar or bank password, and it does not connect to any such account on your behalf. It works from statement files you obtain yourself and open with your device’s file picker.

Where a statement PDF is password-protected, you enter that password in the app so the file can be opened on the device. The password is used for that purpose alone and is not stored on our side or transmitted anywhere.

What the app holds, and where

Transactions, folios, holdings, valuations, other assets, SIP details, family profiles, settings and any notes you add are stored in the app’s own storage on your device and in a database file whose location you choose. The app also keeps a small number of automatic recovery points locally so a bad import can be undone.

You can protect the whole database with a password, in which case it is encrypted on the device and readable only with that password. We cannot recover it if you lose it, because we never have it.

The one network request the app can make

To value your holdings, the app can fetch publicly published net asset values and market rates. These requests ask for public price data only — nothing about your folios, holdings, quantities or identity is included in them, and no data is sent to us.

If you prefer no connection at all, switch on fully offline mode in settings. The app then makes no network requests whatsoever and you enter valuations yourself.

Files you import and export

Statement files are read on the device. Reports (PDF, CSV, print), the database file and backups are written only when you ask for them, to a location you choose. We never see any of these files and have no means of reaching them.

Deleting your data

Settings contains a control that clears everything the app holds, on the device. Uninstalling also removes the app’s storage; your separately saved database file and backups remain yours to delete. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request the deletion of from us.

What the app is not

Portfolio Tracker arranges your own data and computes returns from it. It does not provide investment, tax or financial advice, does not recommend any security, and does not file anything on your behalf. Capital-gains and Schedule 112A outputs are a convenience format built from your records — verifying them, and your return, is between you and a qualified adviser.

Children

Portfolio Tracker is a financial record-keeping utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that sends anything about your portfolio anywhere, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the change described plainly here.
