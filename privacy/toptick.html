Privacy policy

TopTick privacy policy.

Applies to TopTick for Android (package com.tekzova.toptick). Last updated 15 October 2026.

The short version: TopTick does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data. It has no internet permission and is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK of any kind in the app.

Where your data lives

Every checklist, item, tick, quantity, reminder and setting is written to your own device’s private storage and read back from it. None of it leaves the phone unless you personally choose to send it.

You can verify this yourself: open Settings › Apps › TopTick › Permissions on your Android device. The app does not request internet or network access, so there is no connection for it to use.

Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show a reminder you scheduled for one of your own checklists. Nothing else produces a notification.
Alarms & remindersSo a reminder fires at the time you set.
Run at startupAndroid discards scheduled alarms when a phone restarts, so the app re-creates your reminders after a reboot. It does nothing else at startup.

The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or storage permissions.

Sharing a checklist

When you tap Share, TopTick hands plain text to Android’s standard share sheet and you choose which app sends it — WhatsApp, email, or anything else installed. That app, not TopTick, performs the sending, and its own privacy policy governs what happens next. TopTick never shares anything on its own or in the background.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you ask for one, and are saved to a location you pick using your device’s own file picker. We never see them and have no way to reach them.

Purchases

If you buy the Pro features, the purchase is handled by Google Play under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We receive confirmation that a purchase is valid, not your payment details.

Children

TopTick is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that involves a network connection, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described plainly here.
