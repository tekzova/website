Privacy policy

ZovaScan privacy policy.

Applies to ZovaScan for Android (package com.tekzova.zovascan). Last updated 21 November 2026.

The short version: ZovaScan has no account and no server. Your scans stay on your phone. The app connects to the internet only when you tap Back up, and then only to your own Google Drive.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-in to a Tekzova service, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no advertising code in the app. We never see your documents.

Where your documents live

Every scan, folder, ID card and setting is stored in the app’s own storage on your phone. Finding the page, straightening it, filters and text reading all run on the phone.

Items in the Vault are encrypted on the phone and open with your fingerprint, your screen lock or a PIN you set.

Backup to Google Drive

Backup is optional and starts only when you tap Back up. The backup is encrypted on the phone and uploaded to your own Google Drive, under your own Google account. Google’s privacy policy applies to what is stored there. We have no access to it.

Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy
CameraTo scan pages and read QR codes. Pictures are taken only when you press the shutter.
Fingerprint / screen lockTo open the Vault and the app lock. The app never sees your fingerprint; Android only tells it yes or no.
InternetOnly for the Google Drive backup you start yourself.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or access to all files on the phone.

Sharing

When you share a scan, ZovaScan hands the file to the app you choose from Android’s share sheet. That app does the sending, and its own privacy policy applies.

Children

ZovaScan is a general document tool. It is not directed at children and collects no data from any user.

Changes

If the app ever gains a feature that uses a network connection in a new way, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
