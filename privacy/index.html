Privacy

The shortest privacy page you will read today.

We do not collect your data. Not a reduced amount of it, not an anonymised version of it — none. Our apps have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising code, and everything they hold is written to your own device.

OffGridWealth

No account, no server. Statements are read on your device. The app goes online only to fetch public fund prices or to back up to your own Google Drive — and only when you switch those on.

Two things worth checking yourself.

On Android, open Settings › Apps, pick the app and look at its permissions. TopTick and NRE Tracker request no internet access at all, so they are not able to send data anywhere even if they wanted to. OffGridWealth asks for internet access only so that you can refresh fund prices and back up to your own Google Drive; both are off until you turn them on, and fully offline mode stops them again.

If an app of ours ever needs a network connection for something new, we will say so on this page and in the app before that version ships — not in a policy update nobody reads. Anything we cannot promise honestly, we would rather not build.
