Privacy

The shortest privacy page you will read today.

We do not collect your data. Not a reduced amount of it, not an anonymised version of it — none. Our apps have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising code, and everything they hold is written to your own device.

TopTick goes one step further and requests no internet permission at all, which means it is not able to send data anywhere even if it wanted to. You can confirm that yourself in Android’s app settings in about thirty seconds — and you should, for every app on your phone.

Each app has its own policy, written for what that app actually does. All three say the same thing in the end: your records stay on your phone.

If that ever changes

If an app of ours ever needs a network connection to do something useful, we will say so on this page and in the app before that version ships — not in a policy update nobody reads. Anything we cannot promise honestly, we would rather not build.
