Privacy policy

ZovaPDF privacy policy.

Applies to ZovaPDF for Android (package com.tekzova.zovapdf). Last updated 21 November 2026.

The short version: ZovaPDF has no account and no server of ours. Your files stay on your phone. The app uses the network only for Audience Sync, which sends your slides directly from your phone to the phones in the room.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no analytics and no advertising code in the app. We never see your files.

Where your files live

The app works on the files you add or open with it. It does not ask for access to all files on the phone. Every tool saves its result as a new file; your original is never changed.

Audience Sync

When you start Audience Sync, your phone serves the open document to other phones on the same hotspot or Wi-Fi network. The pages travel directly from your phone to theirs. They do not pass through any Tekzova server or the internet. You choose whether attendees may look ahead or save the PDF, and sharing stops when you stop it.

Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy
Internet and network stateSo attendees’ phones can reach your phone during Audience Sync, and so the app can find a presentation on the same network.
Wi-Fi state and multicastTo show the right address and to announce a live presentation to phones on the same network.
VibrateFor a short buzz on presenter controls.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or access to all files.

Text reading (OCR) and read-aloud

Text recognition runs on the phone with a model included in the app. Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone.

Children

ZovaPDF is a general document tool. It is not directed at children and collects no data from any user.

Changes

If the app ever uses a network connection in a new way, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
