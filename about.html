The company

Software built on one firm rule.

Tekzova Technologies builds applications for people who would rather keep their own records than hand them to someone else’s server. Every product we release is designed, written and tested in-house, and used daily by the people who made it.

Why we build this way.

Most apps are built around a server because that is where the business model lives: the data, the subscription, the ads. We started from the other end. The problems we wanted to solve — knowing what a family is worth, counting days for a residency rule, keeping a shopping list straight — never needed a server in the first place. So we never built one.

That one decision makes the rest simple. There is no account to create, nothing to breach, no monthly fee to keep paying, and nothing that stops working if we ever close. The apps are paid for the old-fashioned way: once, by the people who find them useful.

No account, no server

Nothing of yours is held by us. There is no database of customers to leak.

Offline first

Every feature works with no connection. Online features are optional and off until you turn them on.

Pay once

A 14-day full trial, then one payment if you want Pro. Never a subscription, never ads.

Phone and PC

The same app on both, sharing one backup file. Nothing passes through us.
