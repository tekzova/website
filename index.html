Two people, one shopping trip

Send the list. They tick. You see it.

Sharing is a plain WhatsApp message — readable by anyone, even without the app. But if they do have TopTick, something better happens.

You share it

Tap Share and pick WhatsApp, email, anything. It goes out as ordinary text with the activity’s own icon — ⚓ for a ship joining kit, 🛒 for the shopping.

They tick it on their phone

They copy the message, open TopTick, and tap Import. Now it is a real checklist in their app — quantities, units and all — and they tick things off in their own aisle.

They send it back, and your list updates

TopTick recognises it as the same list and updates yours instead of making a duplicate. Their ticks appear on your copy, with the real time each one was done and who did it. Nothing you already ticked is ever undone.

Before you install

Reasonable questions.

If it has no internet, how does sharing work?
Your phone’s share sheet does it. TopTick hands the text to WhatsApp, Gmail or whatever you pick, and that app sends it — the same as sharing a photo. TopTick itself never opens a connection, which is why it needs no permission.
What happens to my lists if I change phones?
Settings → Backup to file writes everything to one small file you keep wherever you like — Drive, a computer, an SD card. On the new phone, Restore from file brings back every list, tick, quantity and reminder.
Is it really free? What’s the catch?
Free, with no ads, no subscription and no paid tier. There is no catch because there is no business model attached to your data — the app cannot send it anywhere. It was built to solve a real problem, and it is small enough to give away.
Can two of us edit the same list at the same time?
Not live — that would need a server. What you get instead: share the list, they tick it in their own copy, send it back, and your list updates rather than duplicating. Their ticks merge into yours with the real times. For a shopping trip it works well; for second-by-second collaboration it does not.
Does it work offline, on a ship, in a basement?
It only works offline. There is no online mode to fall back from. Everything — creating, ticking, reminders, backups — happens on the device with no signal at all.
Which Android versions does it run on?
Android 7.0 (2016) and newer, which covers effectively every phone still in use. The app is about 3.6 MB.
