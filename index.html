Where your data lives

On your phone. That is the whole architecture.

Not encrypted-in-transit, not stored-securely, not anonymised. Never sent.

Nothing to breach

No server holds your lists, your contracts or your holdings — because there is no server. There is nothing of yours for anyone to lose.

Works with no signal

Mid-ocean, in a basement, on a plane. There is no online mode to fall back from, so there is nothing to fall back to.

You hold the backup

Every app writes a file you keep, wherever you like. A new phone is a thirty-second restore, not an evening of retyping.

Questions

Before you install.

Is it really free?
Yes. No account, no trial period, no feature held back. If paid features are added later they will be a one-time purchase and nothing that is free today will move behind them.
Why do two apps install from a file instead of Google Play?
They are finished and in daily use but have not been through store review yet. The APK on each app page is the same build, straight from us. When they reach Play, those pages will carry the store link instead.
What does "nothing uploaded" actually mean?
Your records are written to the app's own storage on your device and read back from there. TopTick goes further and requests no internet permission at all — check it yourself under Settings › Apps › TopTick. Portfolio Tracker can fetch published fund prices if you ask it to, and nothing about your holdings goes with that request.
What happens when I change phones?
Each app writes a backup file you keep. Copy it across, import it, and everything comes back. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to lose.
Which Android versions?
7.0 and newer, which covers effectively every phone still in use. The apps are between 2 and 4 MB.
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
Email info@tekzova.com. It reaches the people who wrote the app, and requests do get built.
