Your data

Nothing is uploaded.
That is the feature.

Most apps in this category are a friendly front end for somebody else’s database. Ours are not. Your lists, dates and holdings are written to your own phone and read back from it — so there is no copy anywhere to be leaked, sold, or lost in an outage.

You still share when you want to: hand a list to someone through WhatsApp or email, and their ticks come back into your copy. The difference is that you choose the moment, and nothing moves on its own in the background.

The fewest taps to something useful.

That is the rule every app here is built against. TopTick shows it best, so it is the example below — three ways to build a list, where most checklist apps hand you an empty box and a keyboard.

Tap from the suggestions

Sixteen activities, 998 items, already grouped. Open Grocery and tap produce, dairy, frozen, household. A full shop in about forty seconds without typing a word.

02

Paste a sentence — it becomes tickable

Someone texts “bread, eggs, coffee, dish soap, bananas”. Copy it, tap once, and you have five separate items with their own tick boxes. Commas, bullets, numbered lines and line breaks are all understood.

Questions

Fair things to ask.

Especially the two about money. Everybody should ask those.

If there are no ads, how do you make money?
From people who choose to buy an advanced feature, once. That is the whole business — no ads, no subscriptions, and above all no data business. TopTick cannot sell your data even in principle, because it has no internet permission to send it with. We would rather be paid openly by a few people than quietly by an advertiser at the expense of all of them.
What is free, and what costs money?
Everything you need to actually use an app is free, with no time limit, no account and no card details. Some advanced features are a one-time purchase — never a subscription. You can try those in full before deciding, and if you buy and change your mind, email us within 30 days for a full refund. Buy once, keep it.
Can two people work on the same list?
Yes. Share it, they tick items in their own copy, send it back, and your list updates rather than duplicating — their ticks, with the time each was done. Because nothing is held online, your list is never sitting in a database waiting to be leaked, and it keeps working in a shop with no signal.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no sign-up screen in any Tekzova app. You install it and it works. Nothing is tied to an email address, so nothing follows you around.
What happens to my data if I change phones?
Each app writes a backup file you keep yourself — on Drive, a computer, anywhere. Restoring it on the new phone brings everything back. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to lose or leak.
How many apps are there, really?
Three have public pages, one of them on Google Play today, and several more are in development. We list an app here when it exists and works, which is why the page grows slowly and why nothing on it is a mock-up of something unbuilt.
Who is behind Tekzova?
A small independent team. The apps come out of real working life — checklists that had to work at sea with no signal, day-counts that decided a tax return, investments tracked across a decade of it. That is why they are practical rather than pretty.
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
Email info@tekzova.com. It reaches the people who wrote the app, and requests do get built — several features in TopTick started as a message from someone using it.
