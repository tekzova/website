Questions

Before you install.

Is it really free?
Yes. Every app's core is free with no account and no time limit. Some advanced features will be paid — you get fourteen days with them unlocked first, and if you don't buy, the app carries on working as the free version. Nothing that is free today will ever move behind the paywall.
How does the 14-day trial work?
Tap Try Pro inside the app. Everything unlocks immediately — no card, no email, no account. After fourteen days the advanced features switch off and the rest of the app keeps running exactly as before. If you buy, it's one payment and it's yours.
Does my phone backup open on the PC?
Yes — for NRE Tracker and Portfolio Tracker, which run on both. The two versions share the same screens and the same backup file, in either direction. Copy the file across however you like and import it; there is no conversion step and nothing goes through us.
Is NRE Tracker really free with no paid version?
Completely free, on Android and on Windows. Every feature, every export, no trial clock and nothing held back. Paid features are planned only for TopTick and Portfolio Tracker.
What does "nothing uploaded" actually mean?
Your records are written to the app's own storage on your device and read back from there. TopTick goes further and requests no internet permission at all — check it under Settings › Apps › TopTick. Where an app can fetch published data such as fund prices, it asks you first, and nothing of yours goes with the request.
Where do the Windows programs come from?
Straight from this site. Each download shows its version, size and checksum, so you can confirm the file is the one we published. No store account and no installer bundled with anything else.
What happens when I change phone or PC?
Each app writes a backup file you keep. Copy it across, import it, and everything comes back. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to lose — and nothing for you to wait on.
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
Email info@tekzova.com. It reaches the people who wrote the app, and requests do get built.
