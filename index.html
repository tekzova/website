03 / Approach

Four rules we don’t break.

Most free apps are free because you are the product. Ours are free because the useful part should be, and paid for — once, by choice — when you want more than that.

One job, done properly

A checklist app should be the best checklist app on your phone, not a note-taker with tick boxes bolted on. We would rather do less and get it right.

Focus

No account, ever

You should not have to hand over an email address to write down that you are out of milk. There is no sign-up screen in any app we make.

No sign-up

Your data stays on your device

Written to your phone’s own storage and read back from it. TopTick goes further and requests no internet permission at all — check it yourself in Android settings.

On-device

Quiet, and never rented

No ads. No subscription. No notification that is really an advertisement. Where there is an advanced feature to pay for, it is one payment, you can try it first, and the app asks once rather than nagging.

06 / Questions

Fair things to ask.

Especially the two about money. Everybody should ask those.

If there are no ads, how do you make money?
From people who choose to buy an advanced feature, once. That is the whole business — no ads, no subscriptions, and above all no data business. TopTick cannot sell your data even in principle, because it has no internet permission to send it with. We would rather be paid openly by a few people than quietly by an advertiser at the expense of all of them.
What is free, and what costs money?
Everything you need to actually use an app is free, with no time limit, no account and no card details. Some advanced features are a one-time purchase — never a subscription, never a monthly charge. You can try those features in full before deciding, and if you buy and change your mind, email us within 30 days for a full refund. Buy once, keep it.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no sign-up screen in any Tekzova app. You install it and it works. Nothing is tied to an email address, so nothing follows you around.
What happens to my data if I change phones?
Each app writes a backup file you keep yourself — on Drive, a computer, anywhere. Restoring it on the new phone brings everything back. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to lose or leak.
Do the apps work without an internet connection?
Yes, completely. TopTick has no online mode to fall back from — creating lists, ticking, reminders and backups all happen on the device with no signal at all. Useful on a ship, in a basement, or on a flight.
Who is behind Tekzova?
A small independent team. The apps come out of real working life — checklists that had to work at sea with no signal, day-counts that decided a tax return, investments tracked across a decade of it. That is why they are practical rather than pretty.
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
Email info@tekzova.com. It reaches the people who wrote the app, and requests do get built — several features in TopTick started as a message from someone using it.
