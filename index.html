Questions

Before you install.

Is it really free?
Yes. The core of every app is free, with no account and no time limit. Some advanced features are paid: you get fourteen days with them unlocked first, and if you don’t buy, the app simply carries on as the free version. Nothing that is free today will ever move behind a paywall. NRE Tracker has no paid version at all.
How does the 14-day trial work?
Every feature is unlocked the moment you install — no card, no email, no account. After fourteen days the advanced features switch off and the rest of the app keeps running exactly as before. If you buy, it is one payment through the store you installed from, and it is yours.
Does my phone backup open on the PC?
Yes, for NRE Tracker and OffGridWealth, which run on both. The two versions share the same screens and the same backup file, in either direction. OffGridWealth can also keep that backup in your own Google Drive, so the phone and the PC share one folder.
What does “nothing uploaded” actually mean?
Your records are written to the app’s own storage on your device and read back from there. TopTick goes further and never asks for internet permission at all — check it under Settings › Apps › TopTick. Where an app can fetch public data such as fund prices, or back up to your own Google Drive, it does so only when you switch that on, and nothing of yours ever comes to us.
Where do I get the apps?
Android apps come from Google Play. OffGridWealth for Windows comes from the Microsoft Store; the other Windows programs will download from this site. Each app’s page says exactly where it stands today.
What happens when I change phone or PC?
Each app writes a backup file you keep. Copy it across, restore it, and everything comes back. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to lose and nothing for you to wait on.
How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?
Email info@tekzova.com. Requests are read by the team that builds the apps, and they do get built.
